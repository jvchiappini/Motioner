## Contexto

Motioner's main differentiator is AI-first authoring with automatic
iteration. That requires sending project context and rendered previews
to cloud providers, and it requires clear approval boundaries so the AI
cannot publish or finalize work on its own.

## Decision

Approved by the user on 2026-10-31.

- Supported providers: Anthropic, OpenRouter and Google.
- Cloud is allowed: project context and rendered frames may be sent to
  those providers, subject to their terms.
- The AI can request preview renders and inspect the resulting images to
  critique and refine its own work.
- The final render requires explicit human approval.
- The AI cannot upload to YouTube; the user downloads the export and
  uploads it manually.
- API keys are stored in the Windows Credential Manager, never in the
  project or repository.
- Iteration runs within configurable time, cost and iteration budgets.
  Defaults confirmed by the user: 10 iterations, 10 minutes and a
  configurable cost cap per task.

## Confirmed defaults

- Maximum iterations: 10.
- Maximum wall-clock time: 10 minutes.
- Maximum cost: configurable per task.
- Human approval required for the final render.
- No upload of any kind by the AI.

## Consecuencias

Gains:
- Automatic iteration becomes feasible with visual feedback.
- Clear human control over expensive and irreversible actions.
- No YouTube OAuth scope or quota management in 1.0.

Costs:
- Project data leaves the machine; privacy must be explicit in the UI.
- Provider differences require an abstraction layer.
- Iteration can be expensive; budgets and cancellation are mandatory.

Pending:
- Default iteration, time and cost budgets.
- Provider/model routing and fallback behavior.
- Whether offline/local models are added later.
