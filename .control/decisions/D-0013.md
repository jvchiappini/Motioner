## Contexto

Motioner will be public and free, and it must eventually generate
revenue. The license must allow broad use, contribution and commercial
activity, and the repository must not include assets with incompatible
terms.

## Decision

Approved by the user on 2026-10-31.

- License: Apache License 2.0.
- The project is public, free and open source.
- Bundled assets must use permissive licenses: CC0, OFL, Apache or MIT,
  with attribution where required.
- Users may import any asset they have the rights to use; Motioner does
  not police user projects.
- AI-generated output is subject to the providers' terms.
- Revenue must come from services or optional products compatible with
  Apache-2.0, not from restricting the open core.

## Consecuencias

Gains:
- Maximum adoption and contribution.
- Patent grant and a clear commercial framework.
- No legal ambiguity for repository assets.

Costs:
- Competitors may use the code commercially.
- Revenue depends on services, hosted features or support.
- Every bundled asset needs a recorded license.
