## Contexto

The pin of D-0019 (`32aea8af74a510c1d89456e861b6dd8dbaeb167f`) lacked the
engine work done since: the Pure2D compositing and polish pass (`df2d4bf`),
the multi-view hard-edge, readback-ring and layer fixes (`0ef6fe6`, which
closes the pending items listed in D-0019), and Part A of prompt 08:
exact sRGB colour constructors, border/shadow packing fixes,
`GuiBatch::draw_text_baseline` with kerning, and an in-app screenshot hook
(`AppContext::request_screenshot`, `FerrousApp::on_screenshot`). The
screenshot hook lets the editor capture itself without `PrintWindow`.

## Decision

Pin `engine/ferrous` to `c4ae1f24fce51499e2818772d2fd87561e202a86` (2026-11-01), the head of FerrousEngine
branch `feat/pure2d-text-and-readback` (pushed, not merged). The
submodule model of D-0018 and its sparse patterns are unchanged.

Motioner changes needed by the bump:
- `motioner_render::frame::map_harness_error` maps the new
  `HarnessError::ReadbackPending` to `RenderError::Readback` (unreachable:
  Motioner never resizes the readback ring).
- The golden still `crates/motioner_testkit/golden/hello_f120.png` was
  regenerated with `motioner still --frame 120`: 14 of 2 073 600 pixels
  changed, all on the anti-aliased edge of the "Motioner" title, maximum
  channel difference 4. The SHA-256 in `tests/golden_hello.rs` was updated.

Checks on the new pin from the Motioner root (`-j 2`): `cargo test
--workspace`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` and
`cargo fmt -- --check` are green.

## Consecuencias

Gains:
- The editor can use exact sRGB colours, correct borders and shadows,
  baseline-aligned kerned text and in-app screenshots.
- D-0019's pending engine defects are fixed upstream.

Costs:
- The pin is still on a feature branch, not on engine `main`.
- Engine crates still emit pre-existing clippy warnings when linted on
  their own (not touched here).
