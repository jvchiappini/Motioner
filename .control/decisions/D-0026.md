## Contexto

Until session 013 the composition was always fitted to the canvas: no zoom,
no pan, and therefore no zoom command in D-0024. Every screen ↔ composition
conversion (picks, bounds, gizmo handles, snapping, safe areas, guides, the
zoom readout) recomputed the letterboxed rectangle locally from the canvas
rect. Adding a second camera would have multiplied those copies and let the
overlay disagree with the composition.

## Decision

Zoom and pan are **view state**, like guides and panel sizes, never document
state:

- `editor::ViewState` gains `zoom: Option<f64>` (`None` fits the
  composition), `pan: (f64, f64)` in composition pixels and `fit_zoom`, the
  fit scale of the current canvas, which the preview updates each frame so a
  wheel step while fitted is relative to what the user sees.
- `viewport::Viewport` is the one mapping. It derives the composition's
  screen rectangle from the composition size, the canvas area, the zoom and
  the pan, and exposes `to_screen`, `to_composition`, `box_to_screen` and the
  fit scale. The renderer is still fed a `ViewFit` built from that rectangle,
  so the composition and every overlay share one camera.
- `zoom(factor)` is absolute (clamped to 5 %..3200 %), `zoom_fit()` returns
  to fit and resets the pan, `zoom_by(steps, x=None, y=None)` multiplies by
  1.25 per wheel step and keeps the composition point under the pointer
  fixed when given, and `pan(dx, dy)` moves the view by composition pixels.
  All four are commands of the D-0024 layer.
- Gestures: Ctrl+wheel on the canvas zooms around the pointer; a middle
  button drag or Space+drag pans; `F` and a click on the zoom pill fit; `1`
  goes to 100 %. Space toggles playback on release **only when it did not
  drag**, so Space+drag stays a pan.
- `state()` reports `zoom` (`null` = fit) and `pan`.

## Consecuencias

- Determinism is untouched: zoom and pan never enter the document, the
  composition plan or a rendered frame. The golden tests stay byte-identical.
- One mapping for drawing and hit testing; the pure `Viewport` tests cover
  fit, 100 %, 400 %, pan and the fixed-point rule.
- The zoom pill and the top-bar toggles now come from `Layout`, so the paint
  and the hit test cannot drift.
- `fit_zoom` is a small piece of layout-derived state in the editor. It only
  affects a wheel step taken while fitted; headless runs keep 1.0 and
  `zoom_by` from a fitted view is still deterministic.
- A zoomed composition is larger than the canvas. The 2D pass draws it
  outside the canvas rectangle, and the opaque panels painted afterwards hide
  it; the overlays are clipped to the canvas as before.
