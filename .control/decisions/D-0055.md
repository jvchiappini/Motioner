## Contexto

The editor, the renderer, the bake and a future interactive export must
agree on what a rig does at a frame. If the semantics live in the DSL
and are re-implemented in a player, the two will drift. The runtime must
also work without the editor, the window or the GPU.

## Decision

- Add a workspace crate `motioner_rig` with **no dependencies except
  `motioner_core`** (pure data). It owns:
  - rig definitions (parts, typed properties, mirror groups),
  - the driver expression IR and its evaluator (bounds-checked, typed
    errors naming the driver and the frame),
  - pose/clip blending, layering, constraints and the state machine,
  - `RigDefinition::evaluate(frame, inputs) -> FrameOutput`, a pure
    function with no clock, I/O or hash-map iteration order.
- `motioner_dsl` compiles MDF driver functions into that IR and uses the
  crate to bake; `motioner_eval` is unchanged. A future wasm player and
  the `motioner rig run` CLI load the same definitions.
- The crate is added to the boundary map: it may depend on
  `motioner_core` only; `motioner_core` may not depend on it; the DSL
  and IO may.

## Consecuencias

Gana:
- One implementation of rig semantics, testable without a renderer.
- A headless path for export players and batch tools.

Se pierde:
- The bake pays for an extra data copy; measured and reported.

Pendiente:
- The interactive player format (JSON/binary compiled rig) and the wasm
  binding are backlogged; the crate is the foundation.
