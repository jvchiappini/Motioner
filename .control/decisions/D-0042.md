## Contexto

The Motioner model (D-0040) must keep improving after release, helped by the
people who use Motioner with their own AI providers (D-0041) or with the
Motioner model. The most valuable signal is what people do, not what a model
says. This ADR fixes which signals are collected, under what consent, how
they are cleaned and when a new model version may ship.

## Decision

### 1. Signals

| Signal | Value |
|---|---|
| The user edits by hand what the agent made (before → after) | Very high: expert correction |
| The user picks one of several variants or style thumbnails | Very high: direct preference |
| The user exports the video that contains the agent's work | High |
| The user accepts the agent's change in review | Medium |
| The user undoes or rejects the agent's change soon after | Strong negative |
| Thumbs up/down, optional comment | Medium |
| Clarification answers (which questions were useful) | Medium, trains §3 of D-0041 |

Signals are stored in the trace format (D-0036) next to the session.

### 2. Consent and privacy

- Collection for training is **opt-in**, per user and per project, with a
  plain explanation of what is sent and why; off by default; revocable, and
  revocation deletes what was not yet used in a released model.
- Nothing is uploaded without that consent (D-0012).
- Before upload: on-screen text, asset content, file names and paths are
  removed or replaced by placeholders unless the user explicitly allows them;
  assets themselves are never uploaded unless the user allows each one.
- Users can view and export what they shared.

### 3. Legal boundary on third-party model output

Responses generated by third-party providers with the user's own keys may be
restricted by those providers' terms for training other models. Until a legal
review says otherwise, the loop trains on **human actions** (corrections,
choices, exports, rejections) and on Motioner-model outputs, and uses
third-party outputs only where their terms allow it.

### 4. Cleaning

- Deduplication, minimum quality (the D-0040 rubric must not get worse after
  a correction), removal of spam and nonsense edits.
- Anti-poisoning: per-user contribution caps, outlier detection, and no single
  user or small group able to shift a release.
- Every record keeps its source and consent version.

### 5. Release cycle and gate

- Periodic retraining (target monthly once data allows).
- A new version must meet or beat the previous one on the fixed D-0040 eval
  set and targets, with no regression in any category, plus a blind review by
  the project owner.
- Versioned models; A/B in the Motioner API; instant rollback; the local tier
  updates through the app's normal update channel and keeps the previous
  version until the user confirms.
- Release notes state what improved, measured.

## Consecuencias

Gana:
- A data flywheel based on expert human behaviour, independent of any
  provider's terms.
- Clear consent and privacy rules before any data leaves a machine.

Se pierde:
- Opt-in and anonymisation reduce the volume of data.
- Infrastructure for upload, storage, review and retraining has to be built
  and paid for; it is part of the cloud cost model (D-0015).

Pendiente:
- Legal review (provider terms, privacy law for EU users).
- Upload service design and storage location.
- The consent UI text.
