## Contexto

Phase 4 adds the safety net: rolling snapshots, retention bounds, a session
lock and a recovery offer on open.

## Criterios de aceptacion
- [x] Committed edits are written debounced and atomically (overrides, 300 ms).
- [x] `backups/` snapshots the authoritative files every 120 s and on demand;
      count and byte limits prune the oldest.
- [x] `cache/session.lock` records the PID and a heartbeat; a heartbeat older
      than 120 s means the previous session crashed.
- [x] On open, a snapshot newer than `project.motion` is offered with Restore
      and Dismiss (status strip); Restore copies it back and reloads.
- [x] Tests cover snapshot/restore, pruning, stale-lock detection and a
      leftover atomic temp file that is ignored and removed on open.
- [x] The gate is green.

## Notas del agente

- `motioner_io::backups`: `BackupPolicy`, `BackupInfo`, `snapshot`,
  `pending_recovery`, `restore`, `begin_session`/`heartbeat`/`end_session`.
- `Host` implements the session and snapshot hooks; `PreviewApp::tick_session`
  refreshes the heartbeat every 30 s and snapshots every 120 s.
- CLI: `motioner backup <project>`.
