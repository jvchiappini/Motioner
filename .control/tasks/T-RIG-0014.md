## Notas del agente

E1 (2026-11-11): `PreviewSession` takes a host-injected `AssetResolver`; every compile (reload, override recompile, write-through compile) goes through `compile_source`. `Editor::open_with_assets`, `PreviewOptions.asset_resolver` and `ProjectHost::asset_resolver` wire the host; `query`, `edit`, `bake`, `corpus` and the tool host inject `ProjectAssets`. The compiler attaches the versioned `RigManifest` (D-0056); `motioner query <file> --rig NAME|all` prints it and `motioner tools --schema` carries its result schema. E2 still owes the write-through corpus and the rig edit tools.


E2 (2026-11-12): `motioner_dsl::rig_edit` plans property set/key/unkey (mirror both/left/right), pose apply/save/delete, clip key/remove/length/play and state/transition/input edits as byte-range edits on the binding block; `Editor::apply_rig_edit` compiles once, compares the v2 manifest, checks the non-rig shape and records one undo step (D-0057). The eight `rig_*` tools are implemented and contract-tested on the mascot demo. Fifteen fixtures live under `crates/motioner_dsl/tests/write_through/rig_*`. Module declarations refuse with a reason (the accepted addendum to D-0036).
