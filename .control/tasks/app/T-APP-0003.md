The goal of the professional-motion job is `examples/showcase_pro.motion`: a
20-30 s 1080p60 piece that uses every new feature. `examples/showcase.motion`
stays as the v0 baseline.

## Design

- Animated colour palettes, self-drawing line art and icons, a shifting
  gradient background, glass cards with shadows, a masked headline reveal,
  per-character kinetic typography in a real font, an image with a cover fit,
  blend-mode light leaks, custom bezier timing and motion blur.
- Goldens: a handful of frames plus one per feature in
  `crates/motioner_testkit/golden/`.
- Render `out/showcase_pro.mp4` and an eight-frame contact sheet.

## Criterios de aceptacion
- [x] Program compiles and uses every feature listed above
- [x] Golden frames with the D-0028 tolerance
- [x] MP4 and contact sheet in `out/`

## Notas del agente

`examples/showcase_pro.motion`: 24 s, 1920x1080, 60 fps, 80 nodes, six scenes
(brand, colour, glass/image, mask/kinetic, motion, outro). It uses animated
gradient/colour stops, trim paths, star/polygon/arc icons, glass cards with
drop shadows, an alpha-mask headline wipe, `animate_chars` in Inter, a
cover-fit image, screen/add light leaks, `bezier(...)`, `stagger`, `wiggle`
and 8-sample motion blur.

Goldens `crates/motioner_testkit/golden/showcase_pro_f{180,330,600,810,1086,1320}.png`
are pinned by `crates/motioner_render/tests/golden_showcase_pro.rs`. The
Phase 1-9 feature goldens already exist in the same directory
(animatable, paths, gradients, blend, effects, masks, typography, images,
timing).

Deliverables: `out/showcase_pro.mp4` (1440 frames, 1080p60, 6.7 MB) and
`out/showcase_pro_sheet.png` (eight frames). The stills are
`out/showcase_pro_f*.png`.
