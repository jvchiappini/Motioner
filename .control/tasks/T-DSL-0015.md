## Contexto

D-0039 section 2: curated design tokens. `theme(name)` and `use_theme(t)`
live in `crates/motioner_dsl/src/theme.rs`; constructors and setters resolve
colour, type-scale, spacing, radius, font and ease tokens. WCAG AA is checked
when a theme becomes active.

## Criterios de aceptacion
- [x] `theme`/`use_theme` plus token resolution in constructors, setters and keys
- [x] WCAG AA check of `text`/`bg` and `text`/`surface` with the measured ratio in the error
- [x] ten curated themes, each with a rendered board under `out/std/themes` and `docs/std`
- [x] unknown tokens report the nearest token name
