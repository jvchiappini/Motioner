# Product requirements and scope

## Overview

This document records what Motioner must do. The 1.0 scope and the
remaining product decisions were approved by the user on 2026-10-31.
REF-0001 describes how the system is built; this document describes the
product. Implementation details that do not change scope are listed at
the end.

## 1. Users and pillars

- Three audiences: non-technical creators working by chat, professional
  motion designers, and technical developers/artists.
- AI-first authoring: chat is the primary path.
- A real editor for fine control, not an afterthought.
- Code is visible, editable and executable by the user.
- Public, free and Apache-2.0, with a revenue path.
- Deterministic, high-performance local rendering.
- North star: After Effects parity except importing AE projects, plus
  Manim-class programmatic animation with LaTeX math.

## 2. Authoring surfaces

- Chat agent with tools, preview rendering and automatic iteration.
- Timeline/editor with AE-style panels.
- Node graph for compositing, as an alternate view of the same document.
- Python-like MDF program with variables, functions, conditionals,
  loops and LaTeX, which executes to build the composition.

## 3. Language semantics (confirmed)

- **Program-first**: the MDF program is the canonical source; running it
  produces the scene.
- **Custom DSL**, not embedded CPython: deterministic, sandboxed, no
  filesystem, network, process or ambient time, with resource limits.
- The evaluated scene carries provenance back to source spans.
- UI edits become structured overrides keyed by stable identity, or bake
  an element; overrides are written back to MDF.
- Orphaned overrides are reported, never silently dropped.
- The AI can edit the program, add overrides, or both.
- D-0003 is superseded by D-0011.

## 5. Confirmed 1.0 scope

Windows only, 1080p-first.

- 2D motion graphics: shapes, text, images, video and audio layers.
- AE-style timeline, keyframes and curves, masks, mattes, precomps,
  adjustment layers and effects.
- Basic node graph compositing.
- NLE: cut, trim, arrange, transitions and sync for video and audio.
- Audio: stereo tracks with volume, pan, fades, EQ, compressor and
  limiter.
- 3D: glTF, cameras, lights, PBR, skeletal animation and GPU particles.
- Program-first Python-like MDF with loops, functions, conditionals and
  LaTeX math typesetting.
- AI chat, preview rendering, visual critique and automatic iteration;
  human approval for the final render.
- MP4 and PNG export at up to 1080p; manual YouTube upload.
- MSI/NSIS installer plus portable ZIP; no auto-update.
- CPU tests in CI plus manual GPU golden frames.

After 1.0: 1440p/4K, rigid-body physics, color grading, advanced audio
mastering, reverb/delay, HDR, macOS/Linux, plugin API, collaboration,
cloud rendering, cloth and fluids, Spanish localization, auto-update.

Implications: 1080p is the target, 4K is deferred, RAM and VRAM budgets
are mandatory, and the app must request the high-performance NVIDIA
adapter.

## 7. AI workflow

- Cloud providers are allowed; project data and rendered frames may be
  sent to Anthropic, OpenRouter and Google under their terms.
- The AI requests preview renders and inspects frames to critique its own
  work.
- Defaults: 10 iterations, 10 minutes and a configurable cost cap.
- The final render requires explicit human approval.
- The AI never uploads anywhere.
- API keys live in the Windows Credential Manager.

## 8. Licensing and revenue

- Apache-2.0; public and free.
- Bundled assets: permissive licenses only.
- Users are responsible for imported assets.
- AI output follows provider terms.
- Revenue, in order: donations/sponsors, paid cloud rendering, paid AI
  credits. Support, training and a marketplace may come later.
- No core capability is paywalled.

## 9. Remaining implementation details

These do not change product scope:

1. MDF v0 grammar and the exact scene API.
2. Override and bake semantics for generated elements.
3. LaTeX renderer choice and supported command set.
4. Interpreter implementation and performance budget.
5. Installer technology (MSI versus NSIS).
6. Audio effect parameter ranges and presets.
7. Cloud pricing and cost display.
8. Engine and GUIMaker pinning and their update process.

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Program-first plus UI round-trip | High | Provenance, overrides and bake model |
| Solo developer and 1.0 scope | High | Explicit 1.0 list and post-1.0 list |
| 8 GB RAM / 6 GB VRAM | High | 1080p-first, proxies, pooling, budgets |
| AI cost and runaway iteration | High | 10/10/cost cap and approval gates |
| LaTeX quality and coverage | Medium | Rust-native renderer with documented subset |
| Apache-2.0 and revenue | Medium | Revenue from services, not license fees |
| Asset licensing | Medium | Permissive policy and user responsibility |
