# Flujo: audio layer playback and MP4 mux

Audio is authored in MDF, never drawn, decoded once into `cache/`, mixed by
one pure function, and used by both the live preview and the MP4 export.

1. MDF `audio(source, in_point, out_point, gain, start, pan, fade_in,
   fade_out)` builds an `AudioContent` node in `crates/motioner_dsl/src/scene.rs:367`.
   The node kind lives in `crates/motioner_core/src/node.rs:1244`; `gain` and
   `pan` are the only keyable channels.
2. The evaluator skips audio when it builds the render plan
   (`crates/motioner_eval/src/evaluate.rs:260`), so pixels and the
   renderer never see an audio node.
3. On import (`crates/motioner_app/src/host.rs:262`), ffmpeg decodes the
   asset to 48 kHz stereo f32 in `cache/<hash>.pcm`
   (`crates/motioner_io/src/audio.rs:80`). The registry keeps the original
   blob and the peak cache.
4. Preview playback (`crates/motioner_app/src/audio_out.rs:31`) mixes the
   document from the current frame with `mix_document`
   (`crates/motioner_io/src/audio.rs:169`), feeds the default output device
   through cpal and exposes the played-sample cursor
   (`crates/motioner_app/src/audio_out.rs:88`).
5. The window starts/stops the device with the transport
   (`crates/motioner_ui/src/app/mod.rs:707`), mutes hidden and non-soloed
   audio layers (`:678`) and drives the playhead from the device cursor via
   `crates/motioner_ui/src/transport.rs` `audio_frame`; scrub plays 60 ms grains.
6. The timeline draws the clip bar and its waveform
   (`crates/motioner_ui/src/panels/timeline.rs:690`); Gain and Pan are ordinary
   expanded content rows and the clip edges move/trim through `Edit`
   commands.
7. Export builds the same store (`crates/motioner_app/src/main.rs:1418`),
   mixes `[from, to)` to a temporary 16-bit WAV (`:1450`) and starts
   `Mp4Encoder::start_with_audio` (`crates/motioner_app/src/main.rs:1531`);
   ffmpeg encodes AAC and `-shortest` keeps the video duration. `--no-audio`
   skips the whole path.
