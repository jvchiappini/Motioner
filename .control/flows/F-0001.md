## Resumen

A keyed content property (fill, stroke, stroke_width, width, height, radius,
trim/dash, text size/colour) travels from the document through sampling into
the render plan and the painted frame, and the editor exposes the same path
as channels, timeline rows and JSON. A path item additionally carries its
geometry through the plan's arena and is tessellated and cached in the
renderer (D-0029). A gradient fill/stroke additionally resolves its stops,
and the renderer either uploads the gradient table or refuses the plan with
a typed `Unsupported` (D-0030).
