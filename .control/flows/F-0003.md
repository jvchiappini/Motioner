# Flow: video layer decode, proxy preview and exported mux

A video layer is authored in MDF, selects one source frame per composition
frame with exact time math, previews through a bounded proxy/frame cache and
exports by decoding the original; its audio joins the Phase 5 mixer.

1. MDF `video(source, in_point, out_point, start, speed, width, height, fit,
   gain)` builds a `VideoContent` in `crates/motioner_dsl/src/scene.rs:428`.
   The node kind lives in `crates/motioner_core/src/node.rs:1332`; `width`,
   `height`, `in_point`, `out_point` and `gain` are keyable.
2. `Document::resolve_videos` fills `VideoAsset` (key, natural size, source
   fps and duration) through `ProjectImages::resolve_video`
   (`crates/motioner_core/src/document.rs:253`,
   `crates/motioner_io/src/assets.rs:400`). `ffprobe` metadata comes from
   `crates/motioner_io/src/video.rs:70`.
3. `VideoContent::source_frame` maps a composition frame to a source frame
   (`crates/motioner_core/src/node.rs:1380`); the evaluator skips the clip
   outside `start..out_point` and emits an image item keyed by
   `ImageKey::tagged` per node (`crates/motioner_eval/src/evaluate.rs:213`).
4. Preview asks the host for the current frame
   (`crates/motioner_ui/src/app/canvas.rs:210`). `Host::video_frame` uses the
   bounded `VideoCache` (`crates/motioner_app/src/video_cache.rs:84`); while
   playing it prefers the all-intra proxy in `cache/<hash>.proxy.mp4` and
   starts a background job with progress
   (`crates/motioner_app/src/host.rs:110`).
5. The painter uploads the frame under the layer's stable key and bumps its
   revision; the renderer rewrites the texture in place and re-registers only
   when the proxy/original size changes
   (`crates/motioner_render/src/shapes.rs:207`,
   `crates/motioner_render/src/lib.rs:686`).
6. The timeline draws six thumbnails from the asset summary, trims and slips
   through `Edit` commands and shows `in_point`/`out_point`
   (`crates/motioner_ui/src/panels/timeline.rs:815`,
   `crates/motioner_ui/src/app/drag.rs:150`).
7. `still`/`render` always decode the original at full resolution
   (`crates/motioner_app/src/main.rs:1450`); `render` muxes the video's audio
   through the same `mix_document` path as audio layers and AAC with
   `-shortest`.

## Diagrama

Not needed: the numbered steps are a linear decode → upload → draw → mux
path; the cache and proxy job are the only asynchronous parts.
